DECALOGUE

High-Spectrum Cellular Automaton Screensaver
I · ONE LAW
There is only one set of laws. No foreign rulesets may be introduced.
II · NO IDOLS
Still patterns are temporary. Cells that do not change eventually dissolve.
III · TRUE COUNT
Every neighborhood must be counted honestly. No false tallies.
IV · SABBATH
Every 7th generation the Grid rests. All cells age but none are born or die.
V · HONOR THE ELDERS
Older cells may be renewed only by the precise attention of three neighbors.
VI · PRESERVE LIFE
A living cell with 1, 3, or 5 neighbors is protected and shall not be extinguished.
VII · FIDELITY
Birth requires exact conditions. Mixed or impure neighborhoods produce nothing.
VIII · NO THEFT OF LIFE
A void cell may receive life only when it has exactly 2 or exactly 4 living neighbors.
IX · HONEST WITNESS
The state of every cell is public. No hidden or deferred updates.
X · AGAINST COVETING
Dense clusters are unstable. Overcrowding (6+ neighbors) brings swift death.
